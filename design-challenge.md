# Product Design Assignment

**Time:** 2–3 hours

## Challenge

Design a web application that helps people manage and understand their day using tasks and calendar events.

A user should be able to open the product and quickly understand:

- What they have planned for today
- What tasks they need to complete
- What meetings or events they have
- How their day is structured
- What requires their attention

How you solve this is entirely up to you.

We are intentionally not providing detailed requirements, user flows, layouts, or feature specifications. We want to see how you interpret the problem and make product decisions.

## Your task

Within the given time, take the idea from problem understanding to a working interactive prototype.

Your solution should include both:

- Tasks
- Calendar events

Beyond that, you decide:

- Information architecture
- Navigation
- Layout
- Task interactions
- Calendar interactions
- Prioritisation
- Day planning experience
- Visual language
- Additional functionality you believe is important

You are not expected to design the entire product. Focus on the core experience that best demonstrates your solution.

## Prototype

At the end of the session, share a working prototype that allows us to experience the key interactions in your product.

You may create the prototype using any tools you prefer. For example:

- Figma
- Framer
- Lovable
- Cursor
- Claude Code
- Codex
- v0
- Any other design or prototyping tool

The prototype does not need to be production-ready, but the primary experience should be interactive rather than a collection of static screens.

## AI tools

You are free to use AI tools as part of your design and prototyping workflow. However, AI functionality does not need to be part of the product you design.

We are interested in seeing how you use the tools available to you to explore, design, iterate, and prototype efficiently.

## At the end

Please spend a few minutes walking us through:

- How you interpreted the problem
- The key decisions you made
- What you prioritised within the time available
- The core experience and interactions
- Your working prototype
- How you approached building it

## What we're evaluating

We care about:

- Product thinking
- UX and interaction design
- Information architecture
- Visual/UI craft
- Ability to prioritise and scope
- Prototyping ability
- Use of modern tools
- Clarity of reasoning

You have a maximum of 3 hours. We care more about the quality of your decisions and the experience you create than the number of screens you produce.
